Extension Profile & Mission

Built for Engineers, Thinkers, &
AI Power Users

LLMs produce hundreds of valuable responses daily, yet traditional chat history disappears into endless chronological feeds. AI Conversation Saver turns ephemeral conversations into an indexed, permanent knowledge asset.

The Frustration

The Ephemeral AI Trap

You spend 45 minutes prompt engineering a high-value data parser or business analysis. Two weeks later, you need it again, but it is buried under 150 generic chat logs across three different tabs.

Manual copy-pasting to text files breaks the flow, loses original formatting, and lacks instant search.

The Solution

Instant In-Place Preservation

AI Conversation Saver places a discreet marker button directly on every assistant response. One click pins the message with tags, categories, and full Markdown structure.

Access your library anytime from the Chrome Side Panel, side-by-side with your active tab.

Architecture

Engineered to Modern Chrome Standards

Constructed strictly on Chrome Extension Manifest V3 for optimal performance, instant load times, and battery preservation.

Chrome Manifest V3

Leverages modern background service workers and declarative rules. Complies with Google Web Store security and privacy standards.

Chrome Side Panel API

Runs as a native companion panel beside ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini tabs. No floating windows or intrusive screen overlays.

Client-Side IndexedDB

Stores conversations, tags, and category taxonomies locally inside your browser storage for instant 0ms retrieval and offline support.

Targeted Content Scripting

Executes solely on authorized domains (chatgpt.com, claude.ai, gemini.google.com). Never injects or reads unrelated browsing sessions.

Syntax & Markdown Engine

Preserves clean code block demarcations, table layouts, and bullet hierarchies ready for one-click export into IDEs and notes.

Optional Cloud Sync

For multi-device synchronization, securely authenticate via Email or Google OAuth backed by row-level security policies.

Our Privacy & Data Pledge

1. Local by Default: Your saved conversations reside in local browser storage by default. Nothing is transmitted to external servers without your explicit account creation.

2. Zero Advertising & Tracking: We do not track your browsing history, sell data to third-party brokers, or install tracking pixels.

3. Scoped Permissions: The extension requests host access only for the specific AI web applications you use to preserve conversations.

4. Export Freedom: You own your data. Export your entire library to standard Markdown files at any time with zero vendor lock-in.
