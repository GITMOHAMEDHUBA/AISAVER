Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use AI Conversation Saver without paying?

Yes. The Free Starter plan allows you to save up to 100 conversations with full access to the Chrome Side Panel, tags, and Markdown exports forever.

How does the $1.99 subscription work?

The Pro subscription is billed monthly at $1.99. You can cancel anytime with one click in your account settings. If cancelled, your existing saves remain safely stored on your device.

Where are my conversations saved?

By default, conversations are stored in your browser local IndexedDB database. Pro subscribers can activate encrypted cloud synchronization across their laptops and desktop machines.

Does the extension slow down ChatGPT or Claude?

No. AI Conversation Saver uses lightweight MutationObservers and event delegation to ensure zero lag, sub-millisecond execution, and minimal memory overhead.
